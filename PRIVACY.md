# 廣東話字幕 (Cantonese Live Captions): privacy policy

The iOS app collects nothing.

- Speech is transcribed on the iPhone by a model inside the app. Audio and text never leave the phone: the app makes
  no network requests and has no server, account, analytics, ads or tracking.
- Audio is held in memory only while it is being transcribed and is never saved. The text stays on screen until it is
  cleared or the app is closed; it is not stored.
- The microphone is used only while listening is switched on in the app.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/DE0CH/cantonese-live/issues.

---

App 乜嘢都唔收集。聲音喺部 iPhone 度由 app 入面嘅模型轉做文字，聲音同文字都唔會離開部手機：app 唔上網、冇伺服器、冇帳戶、冇分析、冇廣告、冇追蹤。
聲音只係轉緊嗰陣放喺記憶體，唔會儲存；字幕清除或者閂 app 就冇，唔會儲存。只有喺 app 入面開咗收音先會用咪。
